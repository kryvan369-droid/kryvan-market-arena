## Market Arena

Market Arena explores a simple question:

**What if tokenized stocks became competitive experiences rather than assets users simply hold or trade?**

Participants construct a virtual portfolio from tokenized-stock simulations, allocate equal starting capital and enter a timed Arena.

During the round, portfolio performance determines each participant's position on a live-style leaderboard.

The experience transforms:

**Tokenized Assets → Portfolio Selection → Competition → Performance Ranking → Leaderboard → Results**

## Architecture

The Market Arena experience is structured around:

1. **Tokenized Assets** — assets available for Arena selection
2. **Portfolio Selection** — participants construct their allocation
3. **Arena Engine** — manages competition state and market movement
4. **Performance Ranking** — calculates portfolio performance
5. **Leaderboard** — ranks participants during the Arena
6. **Results** — determines the final Arena outcome

The current version uses simulated market movement while the architecture is designed to support live tokenized-asset data and Solana wallet infrastructure as the experience develops.
